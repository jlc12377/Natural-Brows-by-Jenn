Permanent brows for thinning or overplucked brows in Franklin, TN

If your brows got thinner with time, or never quite grew back after the 90s, you are in the right place. Permanent brows put the shape back, in a way that still looks like you.

This is a lot of the women I see

If you overplucked in the 90s, I get it. I was just as enthusiastic with my own brows in college, and those hairs did not all come back. Hormones and time thin brows out too. You do not have to keep drawing them on every morning.

Permanent brows add the look of hair and color. They do not grow hair back, and they are not a treatment for perimenopause or menopause. What they can do is give you a soft, finished shape that is still yours when you wake up, work out, or skip the pencil.

Which technique fits

We decide this together at your appointment, after I see your skin, the hair you still have, and the look you want. You do not have to pick before you book.

  • Nano brows ($300+) place hair-like strokes one at a time. A good fit when you want the most natural look, including sparse spots that need strokes that read as hairs.
  • Powder brows ($300) are soft, layered shading, like a well-done brow powder. Helpful when there is very little hair left, or when you like a softer filled-in look.
  • Combo brows ($300) use strokes through the front and shading through the body and tail. Fullness where you want it, with a front that still looks like hair.

Every brow service includes mapping, a blended color, and a follow-up touch-up about 6 weeks later. See all permanent makeup services and prices.

Brow mapping comes first

When brows are thin or mostly gone, the map matters even more. I measure a center line, then the start, arch, and tail from the sides of your nose and your eyes, with a pre-inked string. Level lines keep both brows balanced, and I check how your brows move when you talk and smile. You see the shape drawn on, and you approve it, before any pigment goes in.

Questions about thinning or overplucked brows

Can permanent brows help brows that thinned during perimenopause or menopause?

Permanent brows add the look of hair and color. They do not grow hair back, and they are not a treatment for perimenopause or menopause. If a health condition means you should wait, see who should wait.

I overplucked in the 90s and barely have brows left. Is it too late?

A lot of my clients started right there, and so did I. Strokes and shading can rebuild a shape even where the hair is gone. We map it first, and you approve the drawing before any pigment.

Do you tint gray brow hairs?

No. I don't tint, so gray is gray, and it's going to stay that way. Permanent brows add soft color and shape to the skin underneath, so your brows look fuller and more defined even with a few grays mixed in.

How do you choose between nano, powder, and combo?

Nano strokes look the most like hair. Powder shading fills more softly when there is very little hair, or when you want a makeup look. Combo uses strokes in front and shading through the tail. We choose together at your appointment, after mapping. You do not have to decide before you book.
