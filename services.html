Questions about services

Which brow technique should I choose?

Nano brows suit people who want the most natural, hair-like look. Powder brows suit people who like a filled-in makeup look or have oily skin. Combo brows give you both. You don't have to decide before booking; we'll choose together at your appointment. Compare them side by side.

What's included in the price?

Every brow service includes your consultation, full brow mapping, a custom-blended pigment, the procedure, and your follow-up touch-up about 6 weeks later.

Do you offer microblading?

No. I specialize in machine nano strokes and powder shading, which many artists find gentler on the skin and more consistent across skin types.

Can I pay over time?

Buy now, pay later options are available through my online booking for eligible appointments.
