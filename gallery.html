Questions about these photos

Are these photos edited?

Only lightly: lighting, color balance, and a little skin smoothing. The brow work itself is never retouched, so what you see is what was done.

Why do the "right after" photos look so dark?

Fresh brows always look darker and bolder for the first several days. Healed color is usually 30–50% softer. See the healing timeline for what happens day by day.

Where can I see more of your work?
