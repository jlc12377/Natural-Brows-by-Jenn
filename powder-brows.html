Powder brows in Franklin, TN

soft shading, front to tail

Powder brows use soft, layered shading to create brows that look fuller and more defined, like you've just filled them in with brow powder. I can keep them light and airy or build them up to a more polished finish.

Who powder brows are best for

  • Anyone who fills in their brows every day and wants to stop
  • Oily, combination, or mature skin, where shading heals especially well
  • Very sparse brows that need shape and density

Want some hair-stroke texture up front? Combo brows give you both.

How long powder brows last

Powder brows are typically the longest-lasting brow option, often one to three years before a color refresh, depending on your skin and how much sun it sees.

Healing

  1. Days 1–4: brows look darker and bolder than the final result. That's normal.
  2. Days 5–10: light flaking and itching as the skin heals. Let flakes fall off on their own.
  3. Weeks 2–3: color can look patchy or faded while the skin settles. It comes back.
  4. Weeks 4–6: your healed color shows, and we perfect everything at your touch-up.

See the full day-by-day healing timeline, and how to prepare for your appointment.

Aftercare

You'll get full written aftercare instructions at your appointment. The short version: keep the area clean and dry, skip makeup, sweating, swimming, and saunas while it heals, don't pick, and wear sunscreen once you're healed to keep the color fresh. See the full aftercare dos and don'ts.
