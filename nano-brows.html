Nano brows in Franklin, TN

hair strokes, one at a time

Nano brows use a single, very fine needle to place individual hair strokes exactly where your brows need them. The result is fullness and shape that looks like real brow hair, not makeup.

Who nano brows are best for

  • Brows that are sparse, patchy, or were overplucked years ago
  • Anyone who wants a natural, no-makeup look
  • Normal to dry skin, where fine strokes heal crisp and clean

If your skin is oily or you like a more filled-in look, fine strokes can soften as they heal. Combo brows or powder brows may suit you better, and we'll figure that out together at your appointment. Curious how nano compares to microblading? Here's a side-by-side.

How long nano brows last

Most clients enjoy their results for one to two years, depending on skin type, sun exposure, and skincare. Over time they fade softly, and a color refresh brings them back.

Healing

  1. Days 1–4: brows look darker and bolder than the final result. That's normal.
  2. Days 5–10: light flaking and itching as the skin heals. Let flakes fall off on their own.
  3. Weeks 2–3: color can look patchy or faded while the skin settles. It comes back.
  4. Weeks 4–6: your healed color shows, and we perfect everything at your touch-up.

See the full day-by-day healing timeline, and how to prepare for your appointment.

Aftercare

You'll get full written aftercare instructions at your appointment. The short version: keep the area clean and dry, skip makeup, sweating, swimming, and saunas while it heals, don't pick, and wear sunscreen once you're healed to keep the color fresh. See the full aftercare dos and don'ts.

Questions about nano brows

Can I get nano brows if I have almost no brow hair?

Yes. Nano strokes are drawn in to look like individual hairs, so they work even where there's very little natural hair. For very sparse brows, combo brows can add extra fullness.

Do nano brows work on oily skin?

They can, but very oily skin tends to soften fine strokes as they heal. If your skin is oily, we may talk about combo or powder brows so your results stay crisp longer.

How long does a nano brow appointment take?

About two hours, including your consultation, mapping, numbing, and the procedure itself.
