Permanent brow and lip blushing FAQ

Answers about choosing, preparing, healing, and caring for your brows. For more detail, see the prep guide, healing timeline, and aftercare guide. Still have a question? Text or call (201) 961-4176.

Choosing your brows

What's the difference between nano brows, powder brows, and combo brows?

Nano brows are individual hair strokes made with a single fine needle. Powder brows are soft, layered shading that looks like brow powder. Combo brows use strokes at the front and shading through the rest. See the full comparison.

How much do permanent brows cost in Franklin, TN?

At Natural Brows by Jenn, nano brows start at $300, and powder and combo brows are $300. That includes your consultation, mapping, and the 6-week touch-up. Lip blushing is $200. See all services.

Will my brows look fake?

Not if they're mapped and colored well. Every set starts with measured brow mapping, and I blend pigment for your undertone and hair color. You can go as soft or as defined as you like, and you see and approve the shape before anything is permanent.

Can you work with gray, blonde, or very thin brows?

Yes. Pigment is customized for light and gray hair, and thin or nearly bare brows are great candidates. Powder or combo brows often work best when there's very little hair.

I have old microblading or a brow tattoo. Can you cover it?

Often, yes. Send a clear photo in natural light before booking. If the old work is too dark, warm, or misplaced, it may need to fade or be lightened first.

Mapping and color

What is brow mapping?

Measuring and drawing your brows before any pigment goes on, using a pre-inked string, a pencil, and landmarks like the sides of your nose and your eyes. It sets your start, arch, and tail and balances both sides. See the full process.

How do you choose my brow color?

I look at your skin undertone and depth, your natural and colored hair, your skin type, and the look you like, then blend a pigment that heals to a natural brown. More on brow color.

Can permanent brows turn gray or red?

They can when the pigment doesn't suit your skin's undertone. Matching and adjusting the pigment for your undertone is how I work to prevent that.

Preparing

How do I prepare for permanent brows?

Stop retinol and acids on your brow area two weeks before, stop tweezing, waxing, and tinting a week before, and skip alcohol and non-essential blood thinners for 24–72 hours before. The prep guide has the full checklist.

Who shouldn't get permanent makeup?

Please wait if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, and talk to me first if you've taken Accutane in the past year, take blood thinners, are in cancer treatment, have diabetes or an autoimmune condition, scar easily, or have a skin condition on your brows.

Do I need a consultation first?

Your appointment includes time to talk through shape, color, and technique. If you'd like to chat before booking, text or call (201) 961-4176.

How far ahead should I book before a wedding or event?

At least 6–8 weeks before. That leaves time for healing, your touch-up, and the color to settle.

Your appointment

Does getting permanent brows hurt?

I use numbing cream, and most clients describe it as light scratching or pressure. Plenty of people relax enough to fall asleep.

How long does the appointment take?

About two hours for brows, including talking through your goals, mapping, numbing, and the procedure. Lip blushing takes about 90 minutes.

Do I get a say in the shape and color?

Absolutely. I draw the shape first so you can see it, and we adjust until you love it. Nothing permanent happens until you approve it.

What's your cancellation policy?

Things come up. If you can't make it, please let me know as soon as possible so we can reschedule and someone else can have the spot.

Healing and downtime

How much downtime is there after permanent brows?

Very little. Most people go back to work the same or next day. For about 10–14 days you'll avoid water, sweat, sun, and makeup on your brows. See the healing timeline.

How long do permanent brows take to heal?

The surface heals in about two weeks, and your true healed color shows at about 4–6 weeks.

Why do my brows look too dark, then too light?

Both are normal. Brows look darkest for the first few days, then can look faint around days 10–14 as new skin forms over the pigment. Color settles by weeks 4–6.

Will I have scabs?

You'll usually see light flaking rather than thick scabs, around days 5–10. Let it fall off on its own.

Aftercare

What should I avoid after getting my brows done?

For about 10–14 days: picking, getting brows soaked, makeup on your brows, sweaty workouts, swimming, saunas, tanning, and retinol or acids nearby. See the full aftercare dos and don'ts.

When can I work out again?

After about 10 days. Light walking is fine before then, as long as you don't sweat.

When can I get Botox after permanent brows?

Wait until you're fully healed, about 4 weeks.

Results and touch-ups

Is the touch-up included?

Yes. Your follow-up about 6 weeks after your brow appointment is included at no charge.

How long do permanent brows last?

Usually one to three years, depending on technique, skin type, sun exposure, and skincare. Powder brows tend to last longest. When they soften, a color refresh brings them back.

Are permanent brows really permanent?

They're semi-permanent. The pigment is placed in the upper layers of skin and fades gradually, which is actually a good thing: your brows can evolve as your face and style change.

Can I get an MRI with permanent makeup?

Permanent makeup is generally not a reason to avoid an MRI. Let the MRI technician know you have it, just as you would any tattoo.

Lip blushing

What is lip blushing?

A semi-permanent tint that evens out your natural lip color and softly defines the shape. It looks like a sheer lip stain, not lipstick. Learn more.

I get cold sores. Can I still get lip blushing?

Usually yes, but tell me when you book. The procedure can trigger an outbreak, so many clients ask their doctor about an antiviral to take before and after.

How long does lip blushing take to heal?

Lips peel for about 3–7 days, can look faded for a couple of weeks, and show their soft healed color at about 4–6 weeks.
