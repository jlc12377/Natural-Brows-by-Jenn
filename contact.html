Before you reach out

What's the best way to reach you?

Texting (201) 961-4176 is usually quickest. You can also email jenn@naturalbrowsbyjenn.com or message me on Instagram.

Can I send photos of my brows before booking?

Yes, please do, especially if you have old brow tattooing or microblading. A clear photo in natural light helps me tell you what's possible.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

You can manage your appointment from your booking confirmation, or contact me directly. Please give as much notice as possible so someone else can have the spot.
