Hi, I'm Jenn

I'm the permanent makeup artist behind Natural Brows by Jenn, inside Tennskin Studio in Franklin's Cool Springs area. My favorite part of the job is simple: you leave feeling more beautiful than when you walked in the door.

It started with a pair of tweezers

Honestly, I think this all goes back to college. My roommates would bring boys home, and those boys had no idea they were about to get their eyebrows plucked. I was a little too good at it, and like a lot of us, I was just as enthusiastic with my own. We're all paying for those overplucked days now.

Years later I had my own brows done, and it changed everything. Not just how I looked, but how confident I felt walking out the door every morning without reaching for a brow pencil. I haven't stopped thinking about giving other people that same feeling.

A creative, now working on faces

I've been a creative my whole life and have found different ways to use it. My career has been in marketing, sales, and customer experience, writing slogans, building ads, and figuring out what makes people feel taken care of. Now I get to add a new tool to the box: designing brows that fit each face.

That background shows up in every appointment. I listen first, I map and draw your shape so you can see it before anything is permanent, and I blend color for your skin and hair. And because customer experience is in my bones, your appointment should feel like a treat, not a chore. More than a few clients have napped right through it.

What I specialize in

I focus on natural-looking nano brows and powder brows, plus combo brows that blend the two, and soft lip blushing. I don't want to give you someone else's perfect brows. I want to show you how good yours can be.

Where to find me

Natural Brows by Jenn is inside Tennskin Studio at 443 Cool Springs Blvd, Suite 103, Franklin, TN 37064. I see clients from Franklin, Brentwood, Nashville, Spring Hill, and all over Williamson County.

Getting to know me

What's your background?

I've been a creative my whole life, with a career in marketing, sales, and customer experience. Now I bring that same creativity and care to designing brows.

Why do you specialize in nano and powder brows?

They give the most natural results across the widest range of skin types, and they let me tailor every brow, from barely-there strokes to soft, polished shading.

Do you only do brows?

Brows are my focus, and I also offer lip blushing for a soft, natural lip tint.
